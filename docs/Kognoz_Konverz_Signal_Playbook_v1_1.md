## 1 · PURPOSE

This playbook codifies the signal intelligence behind the 18-month strategy: 35 accounts, two engines, wallet substitution. It answers three questions for every account owner, every week:

1. **Which accounts should be on the list?** (Account selection — quarterly)
2. **What is happening inside them right now that creates a buying moment?** (Signal sensing — weekly)
3. **What do we say, to whom, with what evidence?** (Outreach construction — per signal)

Phase 1 runs this manually. Phase 2 automates ingestion. Phase 3 becomes the app. The taxonomy is the IP; the tooling is plumbing.

## 3 · IDEAL ACCOUNT FILTERS (ICP)

Unchanged from v1, with one addition ◆: for the Learn/Talent universe, **workforce >100K is itself a qualifier** — job/skills architecture pain is near-universal at that scale (the flagship client stories are 150K and ~450K workforces), independent of any live trigger.

**Hire filter:** ≥500 frontline/volume hires per year · operations in target geographies · visible assessment/RPO/recruitment-partner spend · no global assessment contract renewed within 12 months.

**Learn/Talent filter:** ≥3,000 employees or conglomerate structure · transformation trigger within 12 months (or >100K workforce ◆) · CHRO/CLO function exists · family/promoter-led governance is a positive filter.

**Universal disqualifiers:** hiring freeze · distressed financials · competing behavioral-assessment platform embedded within 6 months.

## 7 · SIGNAL-TO-OUTREACH CONSTRUCTION ◆ vocabulary updated

Four blocks, in order: **(1) the signal, cited** — the specific public event, source, number; **(2) the implication, framed** — one sentence of insight for *their* KPI, ◆ phrased in the site's own pain language (§4D) so the click-through continues the sentence; **(3) the evidence, matched** — nearest named or anonymized proof (Kotak/Yes Bank for banks, Wipro/Genpact for IT services scale, Sime Darby/Petronas for Malaysia, Taco Bell for QSR, the 450K-roles story for architecture); **(4) the ask, small** — a conversation or diagnostic; a demo only for Hire when H1/H2/H6/H11 is live.

◆ **Vocabulary:** use the trademarked frameworks by name where they fit — Augmented Intelligence™, The Immersion Index™, DeepSignal, Decision Architecture, Talent Intelligence Layer™, Legacy-to-Longevity, Human-AI Work Spectrum. Retire generic phrasing where a named framework exists (externally, "the Immersion Index" replaces raw "PAMTW"). Always pair AI language with the human-gate line the site leads with: *"your people always making the call."*

**Psycholinguistic personalization:** for the top 2–3 stakeholders per priority account, profile public language (L12) before drafting: metrics-oriented → wallet math and proof numbers; conceptual → the behavioral-science story; risk-framed → bias reduction, standardization, governance. Say it in the meeting: "we prepared for this conversation the way our platform prepares for a candidate." Governance: AI-drafts, human-reviews, always. Same HAC standard we sell.

**Example — L3b fires (conglomerate announces demerger into two listed entities):**
> The demerger filing on [date] means two boards will each need their own answer to the same question within [n] months: who is genuinely ready to lead each business? We assessed the CEO, CXO layer, and high-potentials for a multinational resources group through exactly this passage — an evidence-based read for the board on who was ready to lead each separating business, before the split forced the question. Worth a conversation while the org design is still fluid?

**Example — L13 fires (IT services major, "skills-based organization" in annual report, 200K workforce):**
> Your annual report commits to a skills-based talent model. At your scale, the constraint is rarely intent — it's that roles and skills were never mapped consistently enough to run decisions on. We built a combined job and skills architecture across ~450,000 roles for a global IT services major: one framework connecting every role to its capabilities, live enough to run hiring, development, and deployment on. Happy to share how they sequenced it.

## 8 · OPERATING CADENCE & THE SIGNAL LOG

**The signal log is the atomic unit of the engine.** One row per observed signal: `date · account · signal ID · tier · source URL · evidence quote · decay date · owner · action · outcome`. This is what Phase 2 automates and what trains Phase 3 prioritization.

**Weekly (90 min/owner):** posting counts (H2/H11) · alert digests · stakeholder moves (H5/L1) · log · act on Tier 1 within decay.
**Monthly (half day, team):** rating rationales + MCA changes · re-score (§9) · review signal hit rate; reweight.
**Quarterly:** concall/annual-report sweep (H8/L9/L13) · universe refresh · retire stale signals.

## 9 · ACCOUNT PROPENSITY SCORE

`Score = (2 × ΣTier1 + 1 × ΣTier2, trailing 90 days, cap 10) × Wallet factor × Access factor`

Wallet: 1.5 confirmed / 1.0 probable / 0.5 unconfirmed. Access: 1.5 warm path / 1.0 identifiable / 0.5 cold. Disqualifiers → 0. Combination rules (§4C) → top decile. Rank the two engines separately; top 20 + top 15 = the working 35, next 10 each = bench. Re-rank monthly; correct the weights against the log from month 3.
